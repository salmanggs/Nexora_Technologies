Our story

It started with a problem nobody was fixing

Shahkot had no reliable way to share local news. Announcements moved through forwarded WhatsApp messages, arriving late and half-wrong. So we built an app for it — Ahwal e Shahkot.

That app taught us more than any course could. Real users on old phones and slow connections do not care about your framework. They care that it opens fast, that the text is readable, and that it works the same way every time.

Nexora grew out of that: build things people actually use, keep them running, and be reachable when something breaks. Today we do that for other businesses too — websites, mobile apps, automation and the software that runs a shop or a school.

What we believe

Four rules we do not bend, even when it costs us a project.

You own everything

Code, domain, hosting, store listing and database are registered in your name from day one.

We say no when it is right

If your idea does not need software, or the budget will not cover it properly, we tell you before you pay.

Plain language, always

No jargon in meetings, no jargon in invoices. If you cannot explain it to your staff, we have not finished.

Bad news travels fast

If something slips, you hear it from us the day we know — not the week it was due.

Our mission

To put serious software within reach of ordinary businesses — the shop on the main road, the school with 300 students, the clinic with one receptionist. The tools that big companies take for granted should not cost a fortune or require an IT department.

Our vision

A Pakistan where every town has its own digital backbone — local news that is trustworthy, local businesses that are findable, and local services that are one tap away. Apna Shehar is our first real attempt at building it.

How we work

Working with us, in practice

Most of our clients have never commissioned software before. Here is exactly what to expect so there are no surprises.

01

You explain, we listen

A call or a WhatsApp chat where you describe the problem however is comfortable — Urdu, English, or a mix. We ask questions until we can repeat it back to you correctly.

02

Written scope, fixed price

You receive a document: what is included, what is deliberately excluded, the timeline, and one number. Free, and yours to take elsewhere if you want a second opinion.

03

Weekly visible progress

A live preview link updated as we build. You can click through it any time and tell us something is wrong while it is still cheap to change.

04

Launch, train, hand over

We deploy it, record an Urdu walkthrough for your team, and transfer every account into your name. First 30 days of fixes are included.

05

Stay reachable

After handover you can still message us. Ongoing maintenance is optional — plenty of clients run their own systems for years and only call when they want something new.
