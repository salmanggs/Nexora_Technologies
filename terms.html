Terms of Service

Last updated: August 2026

These are the standard terms that apply when you engage Nexora Technologies for design, development, automation or maintenance work. A signed project agreement, where one exists, takes precedence over anything on this page.

Quotes and scope

  • Quotes are free, and valid for 30 days from the date issued.
  • Every quote comes with a written scope stating what is included and what is deliberately excluded.
  • The price is fixed against that scope. It changes only if you ask for something outside it, and only after we agree the new figure in writing first.

Payment

  • Standard terms are 40% to begin, 40% at the agreed review milestone, and 20% on launch.
  • Long projects can be split into monthly instalments by agreement.
  • Recurring services — hosting, maintenance, automation running costs — are billed monthly or annually in advance.
  • Work may be paused if an invoice remains unpaid 14 days past its due date.

Your responsibilities

Projects run on time when content arrives on time. You are responsible for supplying text, images, logos and any account access we need, and for confirming that you have the right to use material you give us.

We will tell you exactly what we need and when. If material is significantly delayed, the timeline moves accordingly — we will always flag this rather than let a deadline pass quietly.

Revisions

Each project includes two rounds of revisions at the review stage, plus corrections of anything that does not match the agreed scope — those are not revisions, they are our mistakes, and there is no limit on fixing them. Additional rounds beyond that are quoted separately.

Ownership

  • On final payment, full ownership of the delivered code, design and content transfers to you.
  • Domains, hosting accounts and store listings are registered in your name from the start, not ours.
  • We retain the right to describe the work in our portfolio, and will ask before naming you or publishing screenshots.
  • Third-party components remain under their own licences, which we will identify.

Support and warranty

Every project includes 30 days of free fixes after launch for defects in what we built. This does not cover new features, changes you request afterwards, problems caused by third-party services, or issues arising from changes someone else makes to the system.

Ongoing maintenance is optional and quoted separately. You are free to take the project elsewhere at any time; we will hand over everything needed to do so.

Cancellation

Either side may end a project in writing. You pay for work completed up to that point, and we hand over everything produced so far. Deposits cover work already performed and are not refundable once the project has started, but we will never bill for work not yet done.

Limits of liability

We take reasonable care, test what we deliver, and fix defects promptly. However, our total liability in connection with any project is limited to the amount you paid us for that project. We are not liable for indirect losses such as lost profits or business interruption.

We do not guarantee specific commercial outcomes such as search rankings, sales figures or follower growth, since these depend on factors outside our control.

Third-party services

Projects frequently rely on services we do not control — hosting providers, payment gateways, Meta’s APIs, SMS gateways and similar. Their availability, pricing and policies are their own. We will design around their limits and tell you when a dependency carries risk, but we cannot be responsible for their outages or rule changes.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Pakistan. We would always rather resolve a disagreement by talking about it first.
