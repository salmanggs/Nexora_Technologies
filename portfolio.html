Live MOBILE APP

Ahwal e Shahkot

A local super-app for one town. News, a classifieds marketplace, a business directory, jobs, property, food deals, blood donors and doctors — all the things people used to hunt for across a dozen WhatsApp groups, in a single Urdu-friendly app with everything one tap from the home screen.

Type
Local super-app
Platform
Android
Audience
Shahkot & nearby
Status
Live on Play Store

The problem

Everything local lived in scattered WhatsApp groups — one for buying and selling, one for news, one for job posts, someone’s number for blood donation. Information arrived late, copied five times over, with no way to search it and no way to know what was still current.

What the app does

  • Marketplace — shopping and used items, with trending listings on the home screen
  • Business directory — local shops and services, and any owner can add their own business
  • Everyday essentials — jobs, property, food deals, pick & drop, sports, doctors and blood donors
  • Live news ticker in Urdu, plus push notifications for anything urgent
  • One search bar across every Shahkot service, and a light build that opens fast on 3G

Facebook Automation Bots

A complete toolkit that covers the whole cycle — from building an audience with automated friend requests and invites, through scheduling and bulk posting across pages and groups, all the way to the reach tools that push a post until it goes viral.

Type
Automation suite

The problem

Running a page properly is a full-time job. Growing the audience, writing posts, publishing at the right hour across a dozen groups, replying to every comment, and then trying to get any of it seen — nobody has time for all five, so most pages do one and stall.

The tools we built

  • Audience growth — automated friend requests, request handling and group invites
  • Scheduling — a whole month of content queued in one sitting, posted at the hours that actually perform
  • Bulk posting — one post pushed across many pages and groups at once, with images and captions intact
  • Reach & virality — share, cross-post and engagement tools that keep a post moving instead of dying in an hour
  • Auto-reply & moderation — instant answers to comments and inbox messages in Urdu or English, and spam removed before customers see it
