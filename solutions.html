Built for your line
of business

A retail counter and a school office need very different software. These are the industries we know well enough to start from your workflow instead of a blank page.

Retail Stores

Kirana stores, clothing shops, mobile shops, pharmacies — anywhere stock moves across a counter and a khata register is doing work a computer should do.

Problems we solve

  • Stock counts that never match the register
  • Customer udhaar tracked on paper
  • No idea which products actually make money

What we build

  • POS with barcode scanning and printed receipts
  • Digital khata with payment reminders
  • Daily sales summary on your phone at closing

Schools & Academies

From a 200-student academy to a full campus — admissions, fees, attendance and results, with parents kept in the loop automatically.

Problems we solve

  • Fee defaulters found a month too late
  • Result cards prepared by hand every term
  • Parents who only hear from school at exam time

What we build

  • Student records, fee vouchers and receipts
  • Attendance with same-day parent SMS or WhatsApp
  • Exam marks in, printed result cards out

Healthcare

Clinics, labs and pharmacies. Patient records that are findable in seconds, and appointment lists that do not depend on one person’s memory.

Problems we solve

  • Patient history spread across paper files
  • Waiting rooms with no real queue
  • No-shows nobody followed up on

What we build

  • Patient records with visit and prescription history
  • Online appointment booking with reminders
  • Lab report delivery by WhatsApp link

Manufacturing

Factories and production units around Faisalabad and beyond — raw material in, finished goods out, and a number you can trust at every step in between.

Problems we solve

  • Production tracked in separate registers
  • Wastage nobody can quantify
  • Labour and piece-rate calculated by hand

What we build

  • Raw material and finished goods inventory
  • Batch and production-order tracking
  • Worker attendance and piece-rate payroll

Logistics & Delivery

Courier services, transport companies and anyone running a delivery fleet. Where is the parcel, who has it, and when will it arrive.

Problems we solve

  • Customers calling to ask where their order is
  • Cash-on-delivery reconciliation taking days
  • Rider assignments made over the phone

What we build

  • Consignment tracking with a public status link
  • Rider app with proof of delivery
  • COD ledger that closes at the end of each day

Startups

You have an idea and limited runway. We build the smallest version that proves whether people want it, before you spend everything on the full thing.

Problems we solve

  • A year of building before a single user sees it
  • Agencies quoting for features you do not need yet
  • No technical co-founder to sanity-check decisions

What we build

  • An MVP scoped down to what actually tests the idea
  • Landing page and waitlist before the product exists
  • Analytics so you can see what users really do

Enterprises

Established companies with systems that already work — and one department still running on a spreadsheet nobody wants to touch.

Problems we solve

  • Legacy software the original developer abandoned
  • Systems that do not talk to each other
  • Manual reporting that eats a week every month

What we build

  • Integrations between the tools you already pay for
  • Internal dashboards and automated reporting
  • Migration off legacy systems, without downtime
