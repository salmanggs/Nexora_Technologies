Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information Nexora Technologies collects through this website, why we collect it, and what we do with it. It is written in plain language on purpose.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us. That means:

  • Details you type into the contact or quote forms — your name, phone number, email address, and whatever you write in the message.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends automatically, such as your approximate location and device type, if analytics is enabled on the site.

We do not ask for and do not want financial details, identity documents or passwords through this website. Please never send those through a web form.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To keep a record of what was discussed and agreed, if you become a client.
  • To understand which parts of the site are useful, in aggregate.

We do not send marketing newsletters, and we do not add you to any mailing list because you contacted us.

Where your message goes

Depending on how you submit it, your enquiry reaches us either as a WhatsApp message or by email. In both cases it is read by the Nexora team and nobody else. If you use the WhatsApp option, WhatsApp’s own privacy terms also apply to that message.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not publish client names or project details as case studies without asking you first.

The only exception is where we are legally required to disclose information by a competent authority.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are kept for up to 12 months in case you come back, then deleted. Records relating to actual projects are kept for as long as we work together, plus a reasonable period afterwards for accounting and support purposes.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If website analytics is enabled, it records anonymous, aggregated visit statistics only — it cannot identify you personally.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask us to delete it. Message us on the contact details below and we will action it, normally within a few days.

Client data in projects we build

This policy covers this website. When we build software for you, your users’ data belongs to you, sits in systems registered in your name, and is governed by whatever agreement we sign for that project — not by this page.

This policy is provided in good faith as a plain-language summary of our practices. It is not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for a lawyer-reviewed policy if your business has specific regulatory obligations.
